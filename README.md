### Selected work

- **Materials Project Harmonic Phonon Dataset** ([ChemRxiv](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15004632/v1), under review at *Scientific Data*; [Zenodo v1.2](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24510482)): harmonic phonons of 29,615 inorganic crystals computed with VASP 6.4.2 (PBEsol) and Pheasy on NERSC Perlmutter and NREL Kestrel.
- **Atomate2 and Pheasy workflows** ([Digital Discovery 2025](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5DD00019J)): harmonic and finite-temperature phonons, thermal expansion, anisotropic Debye-Waller factors, MACE-Field Born charges, and polar dipole-dipole rotational invariance.
- **MLIP phonon benchmarks** ([mlip-phonon-benchmarks](https://github.com/hrushikesh-s/mlip-phonon-benchmarks)): finite-temperature phonons from 100 to 900 K with MACE potentials compared against ab initio MD, plus MACE-Field Born charge and dielectric checks against DFPT.
